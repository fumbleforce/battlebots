# Woodland scanned material credits

CC0 photoscanned PBR sets from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/license: CC0,
including redistribution and commercial use), retrieved 2026-09-23 for #34.
Each set is `<id>_diff` (sRGB albedo), `<id>_nor` (OpenGL normal) and `<id>_arm`
(R ambient occlusion, G roughness, B metallic) JPGs. Shaders blend and tint them
with procedural masks; they are material detail, not layout or design.
